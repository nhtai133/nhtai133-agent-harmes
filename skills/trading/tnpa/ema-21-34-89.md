# TNPA EMA 21/34/89 Rules

## Purpose

This file defines the EMA trend-structure component for TNPA Trading Skill v1.

Main skill:

- `skills/trading/tnpa/skill.md`

## EMA Inputs

TNPA trend structure uses:

- `EMA21`
- `EMA34`
- `EMA89`

These EMAs define whether the market has bullish structure, bearish structure, or unclear structure.

## Timeframe Context

Main context timeframes:

- `H4`
- `H1`
- Higher timeframes above `H4`

Lower timeframe usage:

- `M15` is only for signal detection.
- `M15` signals must align with the higher-timeframe trend.

Hard rule:

- Do not trade against the `H4` trend.

## Bull Trend Structure

Bull trend requires:

- EMA21 > EMA34.
- EMA34 > EMA89.
- Price closes above the EMA structure.

Interpretation:

- EMA21 is the fast trend line.
- EMA34 is the middle trend line.
- EMA89 is the slower trend line.
- Price closing above the stacked EMA structure supports bullish context.

## Bear Trend Structure

Bear trend requires:

- EMA21 < EMA34.
- EMA34 < EMA89.
- Price closes below the EMA structure.

Interpretation:

- EMA21 is below EMA34.
- EMA34 is below EMA89.
- Price closing below the stacked EMA structure supports bearish context.

## Invalid EMA Context

Do not validate a trade setup when:

- Price is trapped between EMA21 and EMA34.
- EMA21, EMA34, and EMA89 are mixed or not clearly stacked.
- Price closes inside the EMA structure.
- M15 signal conflicts with H1, H4, or higher timeframe trend.
- The proposed direction trades against H4 trend.

## Agent Checklist

Before accepting a TNPA setup, an AI agent should ask:

1. What is the H4 trend?
2. Is the proposed setup aligned with H4?
3. Are EMA21, EMA34, and EMA89 stacked in the proposed direction?
4. Did price close on the correct side of the EMA structure?
5. Is price trapped between EMA21 and EMA34?
6. Is this a lower-timeframe signal that conflicts with higher-timeframe trend?

If any required answer fails, the setup is not valid under TNPA Trading Skill v1.
